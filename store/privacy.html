Construction Calc – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What we collect

No personal information. Construction Calc has no sign-in and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or anything you type.

Numbers you type

Measurements and settings you enter are saved only on your own phone so they are still there next time you open the app. They never leave your device.

App updates

When the app opens with an internet connection, it asks Expo (the service that delivers app updates) whether a newer version is available. That request includes the app version, the type of phone (iPhone or Android), a random install ID the app makes up for itself, and, like any internet request, your IP address. It does not include your measurements or anything that identifies you. Expo uses this only to deliver updates. Without a connection, the app works normally and skips the check.

No ads, accounts or tracking

The app has no ads, no sign-in, no analytics and does not track you across other apps or websites. It works without an internet connection.

Permissions

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the app's Google Play listing.
