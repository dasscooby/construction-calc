Construction Calc – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Construction Calc does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.

Numbers you type

Measurements and settings you enter are saved only on your own phone so they are still there next time you open the app. They never leave your device.

No ads, accounts or tracking

The app has no ads, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. It works without an internet connection.

Permissions

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the app's Google Play listing.
