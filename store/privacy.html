Construction Calc – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Short version

No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and we don't sell anything about you. Your jobs and numbers stay on your phone. A few features send something over the internet only when you use them: reading a plan, pour-day weather, and checking for app updates. They're explained below.

Jobs and numbers you type

Measurements, jobs, bids, signatures, photos you add to a job, prices and settings are saved only on your own phone. We don't have a copy and can't see them.

Reading a plan

When you choose to read a plan (scan, photo, PDF or file), the image or file you picked is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted by Netlify), which passes it to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to find walls, footings, piers and slabs. The answer comes back to your phone. We don't keep the plan, and it isn't used to identify you. Don't read plans you aren't allowed to share with a service provider.

Pour-day weather

When you tap Check for weather on a job, the job's address is sent to our server, which looks up its location with OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and gets the forecast from Open-Meteo. The address is used only for that lookup and isn't stored by us. The app does not use your phone's GPS location.

App updates

When the app opens with an internet connection, it asks Expo (the service that delivers app updates) whether a newer version is available. That request includes the app version, the type of phone (iPhone or Android), a random install ID the app makes up for itself, and, like any internet request, your IP address. It doesn't include your jobs or anything that identifies you. Expo uses this only to deliver updates.

Texts to your supplier

Ordering concrete opens your phone's own Messages app with the order filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and it doesn't go through us.

Permissions

Camera: only to scan plans and paperwork or take photos for a job, when you choose to. Photos: only the pictures you pick. The app does not use your location, microphone or contacts.

No ads, accounts or tracking

The app has no ads, no sign-in and no analytics, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites. The calculators work without an internet connection.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Deleting a job removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. We don't hold any account or data about you to delete.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
