Construction Calc – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version

There is no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Your jobs, measurements, bids, photos and signatures are saved on your own phone. Two features send something over the internet, only when you use them: reading a plan, and the pour-day weather. They are described below.

What stays on your phone

Measurements, jobs, bids and bills, customer names and addresses you type, signatures, scanned plan pages, job photos, your logo and your settings are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us. If you share a crew sheet, bid or bill, it goes only where you send it (text, email, Notes and so on).

Reading a plan

When you tap to read a plan, the page you scanned or picked is sent to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude AI to find the walls, footings, piers and slabs. The answer comes back to your phone for you to check. We do not keep the page on our server. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms (anthropic.com/legal). Don't use plan reading on a page you're not allowed to share.

Pour-day weather

When a job has an address and you look at its weather, the address is sent to our server, which asks OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service where it is and Open-Meteo for the forecast. Nothing else about the job is sent.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan a plan, and your photo library only when you pick a plan, job photos or your logo. What you scan or pick is saved in the app on your phone.

App updates

When the app opens with an internet connection, it asks Expo (the service that delivers app updates) whether a newer version is available. That request includes the app version, the type of phone, a random install ID the app makes up for itself, and, like any internet request, your IP address. It does not include your jobs or measurements. Without a connection, the app works normally and skips the check.

No ads, accounts or tracking

The app has no ads, no sign-in, no analytics and does not track you across other apps or websites. Everything except plan reading and the weather works with no signal.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
