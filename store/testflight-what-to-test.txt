NEW IN THIS BUILD
- Price book (Settings): your usual prices. "Fill in from job" on a bid now prices it for you.
- Customer signs the bid with a finger, in the job under Bid and bill. The signature prints on the bid.
- Change orders: extra work after the bid. Print each one to sign (or sign it on the phone). They go on the final bill.
- Order concrete: in a job, pick PSI, how it goes in, and when. One tap texts your supplier (add their number in Settings).
- Pour-day weather: in a job with an address, tap Check for the 7-day forecast with hot, cold, rain and wind warnings.
- Read a plan now finds walls, footings, piers and steps too. "Add everything to the job" puts it all in.
- Home screen shortcuts: long-press the app icon for Calculator, Slab Layout, Slab, Jobs.
- Liquid Glass tab bar.

PLEASE TRY
1. Scan or pick a plan in a job, Add everything, then Fill in from job and make a Bid.
2. Have someone sign the bid, add a change order, then make the Final bill.
3. Text a concrete order to yourself (put your own number as the supplier).
4. Long-press the app icon and try each shortcut.

TELL US
- Anything that closes the app or shows "Something broke".
- Numbers that don't match what you'd order on the job.
