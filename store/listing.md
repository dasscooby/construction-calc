## App Store

**Name** (30): Construction Calc: Foundation

**Subtitle** (30): Layout, yards, rebar & bids

**Promotional text** (170): New: build the whole foundation in a few taps. Add-ons, inside walls and slabs figure themselves, with every run listed, a print-style plan, 3D and the bid.

**Keywords** (100): foundation,contractor,concrete,rebar,slab,footing,forms,takeoff,bid,yards,feet inch,stem wall,layout

**What's new:** New: Foundation layout. Start from your house, tap the wall an add-on builds off, set the inside walls by the bay, and tap the bays that get slab. Every run, corner and tee is counted, with yards, rebar, wall forms, a print-style plan and 3D. Also: back up and restore your jobs, and a warning if the phone can't save.

**Description:**

Built for foundation and slab crews. Lay out the whole foundation in a few taps, and the app figures the walls, footings, slabs, rebar and wall forms, then prints the plan, the crew sheet and the bid. Big buttons, easy to read in the sun, and the math works with no signal.

FOUNDATION LAYOUT
• Start from the house: length × width, wall and footing size
• Add-ons: tap the wall it builds off and type how far it comes out. The shared wall is counted once
• Inside walls by the bay, clear, wall face to wall face: 10 | rest | 10
• Tap the bays that get slab. Each one is its own pour, or pour it with another
• Your runs the way you count them: 4 @ 40' + 1 @ 70' = 230'
• Corners and tees handled for you: walls stop at the wall they meet, footings at the footing's edge, an L-bar at every corner and tee
• Print-style plan with every run labeled, bays dimensioned and each pour called out, plus 3D and a typical section

CONCRETE
• Slabs, thickened edges, footings and walls, piers and columns, steps
• Cubic yards with waste, what to order (to the ¼ yard), truck loads and bag counts
• Slabs inside walls are ordered for what you pour, and bid at the size you measure

REBAR
• Slab mats sized to the slab, bars along footings and walls with laps and corner bars, verticals, stirrups, dowels, wire mesh
• A cut list with a cutting plan, sticks to load and total weight

WALL FORMS
• Aluminum panels, fillers, 1' corner pieces, inside corners and ties, wall by wall or straight from the foundation layout
• Daylight basements: wall height that changes around the house

JOBS, BIDS AND BILLS
• Keep every job's takeoff in one place
• Bid and bill lines picked from the job: what it is, how it's measured, the unit, your price book
• A pump line for each pour, deposit and due dates, customer signature on the phone, change orders
• Crew sheet with the load list, rebar schedule and drawings
• Text your concrete order to the supplier; pour-day weather for the job
• Back up all your jobs to one file and restore them on a new phone

FEET-INCH CALCULATOR
• Add, subtract, multiply and divide feet, inches and fractions, answers to 1/16"
• Convert feet-inches, decimal feet, inches, yards and metric; square feet and cubic yards
• Pitch, rise, run and diagonal; hip/valley and jack rafters; rake walls; stairs; circles and arcs; board feet

SITE AND ENGINEERING CHECKS
• Slope and fall, laser grade rod, excavation with swell, fill and base rock, squaring
• Footing size from soil bearing, cylinder breaks, post-tension elongation, form pressure

READ A PLAN
• Scan or pick a plan page and the app finds the walls, footings, piers and slabs for you to check and add

No ads. No account. Your jobs stay on your phone.

The engineering tools are field checks. Always follow your plans and your engineer.

## Privacy answers (both stores)

The app sends only two things, and only when you use them. Neither is linked to the person or used for tracking; both are for app functionality.
- **Plan pages** (photos or PDFs) when you tap to read a plan: sent to our server, which has Anthropic's Claude read them. Apple: User Content → Photos or Videos. Play Data safety: Photos and videos (and Files and docs for PDFs), collected, not shared for ads, processed ephemerally, required only for plan reading.
- **The job address** when you look at its weather: sent to our server, which asks OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Open-Meteo. Apple: Contact Info → Physical Address. Play: Personal info → Address, collected, processed ephemerally.
- **Update check:** a random install ID goes to Expo for app updates (Apple: Identifiers → Device ID, not linked).
