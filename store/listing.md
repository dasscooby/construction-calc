**Full description**
A construction calculator built for foundation and slab work. Big buttons, easy to read in the sun, and it works with no signal.

FEET-INCH CALCULATOR
• Add, subtract, multiply and divide feet, inches and fractions (12' 6-3/4" + 3' 8-1/2" = 16' 3-1/4")
• Answers to 1/16" (or 1/8", 1/4", 1/32", 1/64")
• Convert between feet-inches, decimal feet, inches, yards and metric
• Square feet and cubic yards from your measurements
• Pitch, rise, run and diagonal; hip/valley and jack rafters; rake walls; stairs; circles and arcs; board feet; memory

CONCRETE
• Slabs (including L-shaped), slab + beams poured together, footings and walls, piers (straight or belled) and columns, steps
• Cubic yards with waste, what to order, truck loads and bag counts

REBAR
• Slab rebar: bars each way, laps, sticks to order and weight
• Beam and footing bars, stirrups, a cut list with a cutting plan, dowels and anchor bolts, wire mesh

SITE
• Slope and fall, laser grade rod readings with cut or fill, excavation with swell, fill and base rock in yards and tons, squaring with 3-4-5, decimal feet to feet-inches, vapor barrier

ENGINEERING CHECKS
• Footing size from soil bearing, cylinder break strength, post-tension elongation, form pressure

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Your numbers stay on your phone.

The engineering tools are field checks. Always follow your plans and your engineer.
